# Reading the five systems

## Order and emphasis

| System | Lead with | Then | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Western | Sun, Moon, ascendant | aspects with tight orbs | house claims (no houses computed) |
| Bazi | day master (stem, element, polarity) | four pillars, element balance | ten gods, luck pillars, useful god |
| Vedic | lagna, Moon rasi | nakshatra + pada, current dasha | divisional charts, yogas |
| Human Design | type, authority | profile, defined channels, gates | gate meanings without a channel |
| Thai | weekday | animal year, sidereal sign | predictive "lucky number" claims |

## Phrasing

- Computed fact: "Sun is 10° Capricorn; Moon is 22° Aquarius; ascendant is 14° Pisces."
- Tradition: "In Western astrology, Capricorn is associated with…"
- Uncertainty: "The birth time is unknown, so the ascendant and Human Design profile may move."
- Dasha: "The current Vimshottari period is Saturn, from 2024-06-29 to 2043-06-30." Never call
  the first balance period a full period.
- Human Design: "Generator with Sacral authority, profile 2/4" — a defined channel or center
  is the unit; a single gate is not a type.

## Hard guardrails

- No diagnosis, treatment, investment, legal or safety instruction.
- No deterministic prediction of death, illness, divorce, pregnancy or windfall.
- No "this will happen on <date>" from a dasha or transit; this skill has no transits.
- Do not shame a placement. Describe the tradition's tension as a trade-off, not a defect.
- Do not ask for a full name, ID, address or photo; date, time and place are enough.
- If the user is in distress, stop the reading and respond to the person, not the chart.

## A good short close

> These are traditional readings of one birth record, not predictions. The astronomy is
> approximate at sign and gate boundaries. Use what fits and discard the rest.
